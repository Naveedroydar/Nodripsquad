What We Do

Our Painting Services

From a single accent wall to a full commercial build-out, No Drip Squad handles it all with the same level of precision and care.

🏠 Interior Residential Painting

We paint all interior surfaces including walls, ceilings, trim, stairs, closets, basements, and hard-to-reach areas. We handle drywall and texture repairs, wallpaper removal, stipple ceiling repair, caulking, and more. We use your paint of choice or purchase it for you.

  • Walls & Ceilings
  • Trim & Baseboards
  • Stairs & Hallways
  • Basements & Closets
  • Wallpaper Removal
  • Drywall Repair

🏡 Exterior Residential Painting

Our exterior painting services cover all outdoor surfaces and structures. Built for Ottawa's seasons — we use latex, urethane, or elastomeric coatings as needed and determined together with you.

  • Houses & Garages
  • Fences & Decks
  • Siding & Soffit
  • Doors & Frames
  • Stucco & Concrete
  • Wood & Metal Surfaces
